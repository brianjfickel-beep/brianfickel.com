Focus

Advising is a discipline

Anyone can write a memo. Few people can sit beside a senior leader, read the room and the record, and tell them what they need to hear in time for it to matter. That work can be learned, and it should be taught.

Advising senior leaders

How trusted advisors earn access, frame decisions, deliver hard news and protect a leader's credibility, written from practice rather than theory.

Teaching the craft

I developed and teach a course on how to advise senior leaders. Its lessons, frameworks and case studies will appear here over time.

Practitioner tools

Free checklists, templates and worksheets for advisors and communicators. They run in your browser, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

About

Brian Fickel

Brian Fickel is a communications executive whose work centers on advising senior leaders on strategy, public engagement and the written word. He developed and teaches a course on how to advise senior leaders, has taught graduate courses in integrated marketing communications, and serves his community through local nonprofit and civic boards.

This site shares what he has learned with the advising community. It offers no services.
